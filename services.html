02 — Services

Residential and commercial.

A Vermont based landscaping company servicing both residential and commercial properties.

  • Mowing

    Weekly or bi-weekly through the season, cut height set for the property rather than the calendar.

  • Edging

    Beds, walkways and drives cut back to a defined line so the lawn stops where it is supposed to.

  • Mulch

    Beds cleared, edged, then mulched flat and level to the edge.

  • Hedge & shrub pruning

    Foundation plantings shaped and kept to size, not just sheared flat.

  • Brush removal

    Overgrown edges and scrub cleared back to usable ground.

  • Spring & fall cleanups

    Winter debris off before the first cut; leaves off the lawn before snow sits on them.

  • Excavation

    Heavier ground work handled with our own machine rather than subbed out.

Seasons

A Vermont season is short. Plan it.

  1. Spring

    Cleanup, first cuts, beds edged and mulched while the ground is still soft.

  2. Summer

    The steady stretch. Mowing on a fixed rotation, hedges shaped once growth slows.

  3. Fall

    Leaves cleared, final cut, beds put to bed before the first hard freeze.
